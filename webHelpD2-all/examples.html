Examples

The following examples can be copied and pasted directly into into the graph view of the Analytics-Platform. Open a new Session and use the "Add DID" or "Add VC/VP" button to add the example to the graph.

The examples are mostly for testing purposes and are not meant to be representative of real-world data. They are selected to contain specific relationships to demonstrate the capabilities of the graph. Thus they might also contain errors or inconsistencies, as the platform is still in development.

In case of crashes of the application (yellow bar the button of the graph view), the best approach is usually to remove the pool and the session used and create a new one.
